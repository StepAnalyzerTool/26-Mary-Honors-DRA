FILMING CONTINUITY
Match camera position, landscape framing, actor clothing, room lighting, marker/cap location, worksheet orientation and dolphin. Keep the camera fixed. Keep other people's hands out of waiting/play sections; hands enter only for actual delivery/removal.
Use neutral waiting behavior and clearly distinguish finger tapping from fist/table banging. Do not add claps, spontaneous speech or a visible reaction to praise/stop statements.
Hold the last state long enough for editing. Program-controlled waiting or toy play may need to continue beyond a short source file; the clip ending must never trigger removal or reinforcement automatically.
